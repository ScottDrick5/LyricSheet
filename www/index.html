Paste Lyrics

Paste lyrics you've already written. A title on its own line at the top fills in the song title. Headers like [Verse 1], [Chorus] or Bridge: start a new section, and everything under a header stays together until the next one, including stanza breaks. A note after a header, like [Chorus] (stripped back), is kept as the section's note.

For lyrics you wrote before today. You can only set this now, while importing into an empty song, and it can't be changed afterwards. It's saved as a date you entered: Lyric Sheet's dates come from your device and aren't a legal record of authorship.
